New Papers: design proposals

The popup and the options page in all eight themes, filled with made-up data (titles, authors and journals are fictional). The popups are interactive: switch topic tabs, expand abstracts, dismiss one entry or a whole topic, and toggle switches.

The startup window (windows.create({type:"popup"})) and the toolbar popup use the same 400 px UI. A small toolbar-badge preview sits next to each popup.

Layout

Topics are pill tabs, each with an unread count. Under the tabs: the topic's query, a "Dismiss all" button, then compact cards. A merged preprint and journal article shows as one card with both links.

Accessibility

  • Text contrast is ≥ 4.5:1 and focus rings are ≥ 3:1 in every theme (ratios listed per theme)
  • Visible :focus-visible rings, 3 px in the high-contrast themes
  • Tabs, buttons and switches use real elements with ARIA states
  • Respects reduced motion

Theme setting

Choose one of the 8 themes, or Auto. Auto follows the system and uses a chosen light/dark pair (default: Claude Light / Claude Dark).

Errors

A source failure shows the amber banner above the tabs (shown in every mock so you can judge it). The list is kept and the source is retried on the next start.
